About Me

Hi, I’m Andre Paez-Castro — a graduate student in Information Science at the University of Pittsburgh. I hold a B.S. in Psychology with a minor in Cybersecurity from Western Michigan University, where I made the Dean’s List and was inducted into the Tau Sigma National Honor Society.

My focus is on the intersection of AI, computer vision, and AR/XR technologies. I’m currently exploring computer vision, and immersive applications that enhance human vision.

I build with Python, Flask, JavaScript, C#, and Unity, and I’m motivated by the goal of building innovative solutions that leverage cutting-edge technologies. I’m always eager to connect with like-minded individuals and explore new opportunities in this exciting field.

Projects

Emotion Detection App

This application performs real-time emotion recognition by analyzing a user's webcam feed. It leverages a lightweight ONNX (Open Neural Network Exchange) model for efficient, client-side inference, paired with a minimal Flask backend to serve the application. The primary goal was to create a responsive tool that demonstrates the power of computer vision models running live on the cloud.

This project is deployed on Microsoft Azure and features a full CI/CD pipeline using GitHub Actions for automated builds and deployment.
