About Me

Hi, I’m Andre Paez-Castro — a graduate student in Information Science at the University of Pittsburgh. I hold a B.S. in Psychology with a minor in Cybersecurity from Western Michigan University, where I made the Dean’s List and was inducted into the Tau Sigma National Honor Society.

My focus is on the intersection of AI, computer vision, and AR/XR technologies. I’m currently exploring computer vision, and immersive applications that advance human-computer interaction and mental health monitoring.

I build with Python, Flask, React, and Unity, and I’m motivated by the goal of contributing to AR research. I’m always eager to connect with like-minded individuals and explore new opportunities in this exciting field.
